# Hoppers

A frog race for the bar. The race plays out on the TV, and everyone in the room flicks their own frog from their phone.

## The pitch

- Scan the QR code on the TV. No app, no account.
- Make your frog: a name, one of 12 colours, and a hat.
- When it's your turn, the phone shouts **FLICK!** Swipe your frog up the screen.
- Your frog hops along a track of beer coasters on the big screen. First to the finish wins.
- Land on another frog and you ride it. Frogs sometimes turn around and hop the wrong way.
- A flick at just the right strength lands in the green and wins a bonus hop.

Anyone can play: a tap or a lazy flick still hops. Bots fill out a small crowd, so two people still get a full race.

## How a night runs

- **Lobby.** The TV shows a big QR code and a pond. Each new frog pops onto a lily pad as it joins.
- **Races.** A night is a few races (3 by default). Each starts with a "Get ready" countdown, then frogs take turns. You get 8 seconds to flick before your frog hops by itself.
- **Heats.** A big crowd races in heats (10 frogs each by default), and the last round is a ladder final.
- **Scoring.** Every place scores: 11 points for 1st, then 9, 8, 7 and on down. Heat winners wear a prize rosette.
- **Results.** After each race the TV shows results, then the standings, with rows bouncing in and movers flashing gold.
- **The end.** Most points wins the night. The champion's frog cheers on the TV and confetti falls. Everyone's phone shows their place and asks for a star rating.

A race takes about 2–3 minutes. A night with 8 people is roughly 20 minutes, including breaks between races.

The host runs the night with one button, from a laptop or the bar's iPad: **Start**, **Next heat**, **Next round**, **Final scores**. In between, the race runs itself.

## Moments that look good on camera

- **FLICK!** The phone's screen fills with the word FLICK!, your frog and a power meter. The meter fills as the thumb moves, then the frog flies off the top of the screen.
- **A perfect flick.** The phone flashes gold and says **Perfect!** On the TV, the frog glows gold, a ring of gold stars bursts out, and "Perfect!" pops up over it.
- **The hop.** Frogs squash, launch, bounce from coaster to coaster, and count the hops out loud: 1… 2… +3.
- **Stacks.** A frog lands on another and rides it. Towers of frogs ride each other up the track.
- **Specials.** Green launch pads fling a frog forward in a high arc. Blue chutes slide it back down.
- **The finish line** is a little toy Gravity Brewing brewery. When a frog wins, its lights come on, the roll-up door opens on a glowing taproom, the window lights up with OPEN, and confetti falls.
- **The leader** has flies buzzing round its head.
- **The standings,** Mario Kart style, with ▲ and ▼ arrows for who moved.

## Look and feel

- **Art.** Hand-drawn "ink and marker" cartoon frogs with wobbly outlines, sleepy heavy-lidded eyes, warts and red-tipped toes. The frogs blink, breathe and bob their hats.
- **Hats and looks.** Crown, cowboy hat, beanie, party hat, viking helmet, propeller cap, top hat, daisy, pint, chef hat, sweatband, bucket hat and antlers. There are also looks named after the regulars: Parylene, Appraiser, COTY, Cat herder, Butler, Dreamer, Welder and PM guy.
- **Scenes.**
  - **The bar:** a wooden bar top, a back bar of bottles, string lights, and windows looking out on the Colorado Front Range at golden hour.
  - **The lake:** lily pads at sunset, cattails, a dock to start from, and the mountains on the far shore.
- **Bot frogs** have pun names: Sir Hops-a-Lot, Lily Padsworth, Big Croaker, Ribbit Ross, Toadally Tina, Puddle Jumper, Swamp Thing, Leapin' Larry.
- **Screen style** is dark navy panels with coral for action, gold for scores and winners, and cream text.
- **Fonts** are Bungee, a big blocky display face for names and headlines, and Exo 2 for body text.
- **Motion** is bouncy and playful: titles wiggle, names pop in, winners wobble.

## The words to use

- The game is **Hoppers**.
- The verb is **flick**. You **flick your frog**.
- The track is made of **beer coasters**.
- The finish is the **brewery**.
- A night has **races**. Big crowds race in **heats** and a **final**.
