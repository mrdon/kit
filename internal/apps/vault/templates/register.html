Reset your master password

You're picking a new master password. Your old keys will be replaced and your account will lose vault access until an admin re-grants it. Stored secrets are not touched — they're protected by a workspace key admins can re-share with you.

What happens after you save:

  1. An admin sees a card asking them to re-grant your access. They'll verify your new public-key fingerprint with you out-of-band before approving.
  2. If this reset wasn't you, you'll see a "Reset triggered" briefing on your stack with a link to cancel within 24 hours.
  3. Once an admin re-grants, you can unlock with your new master password.

At least 14 characters. Kit cannot recover it — only your teammates can re-grant access if you forget.

Set up your vault

The vault is where your team stores shared secrets — API keys, service credentials, anything you'd otherwise paste into a wiki or DM.

It's end-to-end encrypted: even Kit can't read what's inside. That's why you're picking a master password here, separate from your Slack login. It only ever lives in your head and on this device — Kit never sees it.

If you lose it, you can reset to a new one at any time from the unlock page — your stored secrets are never lost, and an admin re-grants you access after the reset.

At least 14 characters. Kit cannot recover it — only your teammates can re-grant access if you forget.
